# Tonique

Tonique is a Digital Audio Workstation (DAW) for arranging audio. Use it to
lay out audio files on a timeline, edit and mix them on tracks, and shape
them with effects.

> **Work in progress.** Tonique is still under development. Features can
> change between versions, and this guide covers what is available now.

## What you can do

- **Browse your samples.** Add your sample folders, search them, and click a
  file to hear it.
- **Arrange audio.** Drag files onto tracks, then move, trim, split,
  duplicate and copy clips. Snapping to the grid is on by default.
- **Mix.** Each track and group has volume, mute, solo and a level meter.
  Groups also mix the tracks inside them together.
- **Add effects.** Drop a filter on a track and tune it while you watch the
  spectrum.
- **Loop and play in time.** Set a loop region, choose the tempo and turn on
  the metronome.
- **Save projects.** Projects are small `.tonique` files that point to your
  audio files.
- **Make it yours.** Choose a theme, scale the interface, rebind every
  shortcut and pick your audio device.

Not available yet: recording, MIDI, automation and plug-ins (VST).

# Browsing and previewing audio

The **Files** tab of the browser gives you access to your sample folders.
If the browser is hidden, press **Ctrl+B**.

## Sample folders

- **Add a folder**: click the **folder +** button and pick a folder. Its
  name appears as a button in the row at the top.
- **Switch folders**: click a folder's button.
- **Remove a folder**: right-click its button and choose **Delete**. This
  only removes it from the list. The files on your disk are not touched.

Tonique remembers your folders between sessions.

## Navigating

- Click a folder in the list to open or close it.
- When the browser has focus (click inside it first), **↑** and **↓** move
  the selection and **Enter** opens the selected item.

## Searching

Type in the **Search** box to find files by name anywhere in the current
folder, including subfolders. Clear the box to return to the folder view.

## Previewing

Tonique can open **WAV**, **MP3** and **OGG** files. Audio files have an
audio icon in the list.

Click an audio file to play it. A preview panel appears at the bottom of
the browser and shows:

- the waveform. Click anywhere on it to jump to that point.
- a play/pause button.
- the file name, length and format (sample rate and bit depth).

The preview stops when you start playing the project.

## Adding audio to the project

Drag a file from the browser onto the timeline. See
[Editing clips](clips.md#adding-clips).
